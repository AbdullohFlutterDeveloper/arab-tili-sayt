# 2-dars · Ulanmaydigan harflar va cho'ziq unlilar

| | |
|---|---|
| 📚 **Bosqich** | 0 — Yozuv va o'qish ([[Arab tili Roadmap]] §0.1–0.2) |
| ⏱ **Vaqt** | 70–90 daqiqa · 3 kunga bo'lib bajarish tavsiya qilinadi |
| 🎯 **Natija** | 5 yangi harf · **6 ta ulanmaydigan harfning hammasi** · cho'ziq unlilar tizimi · 10 ta yangi so'z |
| 📋 **Talab** | [[01-dars — Arab yozuvi va birinchi harflar\|1-dars]] — <span class="ar">ا ب ت ث ن ي</span> va 3 harakat |

> **1-darsda** bitta «tish» shaklidan beshta harf chiqarib oldik. **Bu darsda** teskarisi: har biri o'ziga xos, lekin **hammasi bir qoidaga** bo'ysunadigan guruh.

---

## 1. Ulanmaydigan harflar qoidasi

1-darsning 3-asosini eslang: **6 harf keyingisiga ulanmaydi**. Alifni bilasiz — qolgan beshtasi shu darsda:

<div class="ar-blok">ا &nbsp;&nbsp; د &nbsp;&nbsp; ذ &nbsp;&nbsp; ر &nbsp;&nbsp; ز &nbsp;&nbsp; و</div>

Bu — **butun alifbodagi barcha ulanmaydigan harflar**. Boshqa yo'q. Ya'ni bu darsdan keyin qolgan 22 harfning hammasi ulanaveradi.

**Nima demak:**

| Harf turi | Oldingisiga | Keyingisiga | Shakllar soni |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oddiy harf | ✅ ulanadi | ✅ ulanadi | **4 ta** |
| Bu 6 harf | ✅ ulanadi | ❌ **ulanmaydi** | **2 ta** |

> ⭐ Shuning uchun bu harflarni o'rganish **osonroq** — yodlash kerak bo'lgan shakl ikki barobar kam.

---

## 2. Besh yangi harf

| Harf | Nomi | Nuqta | Tovush | O'zbekchada |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| <span class="ar">د</span> | <span class="ar">دَال</span> · dol | yo'q | **d** | ✅ bor |
| <span class="ar">ذ</span> | <span class="ar">ذَال</span> · zol | 1 ta, ustida | **dh** | ❌ **yo'q** ⚠️ |
| <span class="ar">ر</span> | <span class="ar">رَاء</span> · ro | yo'q | **r** | ✅ bor |
| <span class="ar">ز</span> | <span class="ar">زَاي</span> · zoy | 1 ta, ustida | **z** | ✅ bor |
| <span class="ar">و</span> | <span class="ar">وَاو</span> · vov | yo'q | **w** | ⚠️ farqli |

### Ikki juftlikka diqqat

<span class="ar">د</span> va <span class="ar">ذ</span> — shakli **bir xil**, faqat nuqta. Xuddi <span class="ar">ب ت ث</span> kabi.
<span class="ar">ر</span> va <span class="ar">ز</span> — shakli **bir xil**, faqat nuqta. Ular <span class="ar">د ذ</span> dan **pastga tushishi** bilan farq qiladi.

<div class="ar-blok">د ← ذ &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; ر ← ز</div>

---

## 3. <span class="ar">ذ</span> — 1-darsdagi <span class="ar">ث</span> ning jufti

1-darsda <span class="ar">ث</span> ni o'rgandik: til uchi tishlar **orasiga**, havo chiqadi — inglizcha *think*.

<span class="ar">ذ</span> — **aynan o'sha holat**, faqat ovoz bilan: inglizcha *this*.

| Harf | Ovoz bormi | Inglizcha misol |
|---|---|---|
| <span class="ar">ث</span> | ❌ yo'q (shivirlash) | *think* |
| <span class="ar">ذ</span> | ✅ bor | *this* |

O'zbek tilida arabcha <span class="ar">ذ</span> odatda **«z»** bilan yozilgan:

| Arabcha | To'g'ri tovush | O'zbekchada |
|---|---|---|
| <span class="ar">ذَنْب</span> | dhanb | **z**anb |
| <span class="ar">أَذَان</span> | adhān | a**z**on |

> ⚠️ «z» deb o'qish — o'zbek odati. <span class="ar">ذ</span> va <span class="ar">ز</span> arab tilida **butunlay boshqa** ikki harf. Ularni bir xil o'qisangiz, so'z ma'nosi buziladi.

### <span class="ar">و</span> — o'zbekcha «v» emas

<span class="ar">و</span> undosh bo'lganda inglizcha **w** kabi: lablar **yumaloqlanadi**, tishga tegmaydi.
O'zbekcha «v» da pastki lab yuqori tishga tegadi — bu **boshqa tovush**.

---

## 4. Ikki shakl

| Harf | Alohida | Oxirida |
|---|---|---|
| dol | <span class="ar">د</span> | <span class="ar">ـد</span> |
| zol | <span class="ar">ذ</span> | <span class="ar">ـذ</span> |
| ro | <span class="ar">ر</span> | <span class="ar">ـر</span> |
| zoy | <span class="ar">ز</span> | <span class="ar">ـز</span> |
| vov | <span class="ar">و</span> | <span class="ar">ـو</span> |

**«Boshda» va «o'rtada» ustuni nega yo'q?** Chunki bu shakllar keyingi harfga ulanishni talab qiladi — bu harflar esa ulanmaydi. Xuddi 1-darsdagi alif kabi.

---

## 5. So'z «bo'linib» ko'rinadi

Bu qoidaning eng ko'rinadigan natijasi: ulanmaydigan harfdan **keyin** so'z ichida bo'shliq paydo bo'ladi. Bu **xato emas** — so'z hamon bitta.

| So'z | Bo'laklari | Nechta bo'lak | Sabab |
|---|---|---|---|
| <span class="ar">دِين</span> | <span class="ar">دِ</span> + <span class="ar">ين</span> | 2 | <span class="ar">د</span> ulanmaydi |
| <span class="ar">نُور</span> | <span class="ar">نُو</span> + <span class="ar">ر</span> | 2 | <span class="ar">و</span> ulanmaydi |
| <span class="ar">بَرِيد</span> | <span class="ar">بَرِ</span> + <span class="ar">يد</span> | 2 | <span class="ar">ر</span> ulanmaydi |
| <span class="ar">وَرْد</span> | <span class="ar">وَ</span> + <span class="ar">رْ</span> + <span class="ar">د</span> | 3 | <span class="ar">و</span> va <span class="ar">ر</span> — ikkalasi ham |

> 🔴 **Boshlovchining eng ko'p qiladigan xatosi:** bo'shliqni ko'rib, «ikki so'z ekan» deb o'ylash. Bo'shliqning kengligiga qarang — so'z ichidagi uzilish **tor**, so'zlar orasidagi bo'shliq **keng**.

---

## 6. Cho'ziq unlilar — <span class="ar">حُرُوف المَدّ</span>

1-darsda uchta **qisqa** unlini o'rgandik. Endi ularning **cho'ziq** juftlari.

Cho'ziq unli — alohida belgi emas. U **harakat + mos harf** birikmasidan hosil bo'ladi:

| Qisqa | Cho'ziq | Qanday hosil bo'ladi | Misol |
|---|---|---|---|
| <span class="ar">ـَ</span> a | **ā** | fatha + <span class="ar">ا</span> | <span class="ar">بَا</span> |
| <span class="ar">ـُ</span> u | **ū** | damma + <span class="ar">و</span> | <span class="ar">بُو</span> |
| <span class="ar">ـِ</span> i | **ī** | kasra + <span class="ar">ي</span> | <span class="ar">بِي</span> |

**Qoida:** cho'zuvchi harf **harakatsiz** bo'ladi va o'zidan oldingi harakatga **mos** kelishi kerak.

<div class="ar-blok">بَ بَا &nbsp;&nbsp;|&nbsp;&nbsp; بُ بُو &nbsp;&nbsp;|&nbsp;&nbsp; بِ بِي</div>

> ⭐ **Cho'ziqlik ma'no o'zgartiradi** — roadmapda bu alohida ta'kidlangan. O'zbek tilida bunday farq yo'q, shuning uchun quloq o'rganmagan. Cho'ziq unli qisqasidan **ikki barobar** uzun aytiladi.

---

## 7. <span class="ar">و</span> va <span class="ar">ي</span> — undoshmi yoki unli?

Bu ikki harf **ikki vazifada** ishlaydi. Farqlash qoidasi oddiy:

| Holat | Vazifasi | Belgisi | Misol |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oldida **mos harakat**, o'zi **harakatsiz** | **cho'ziq unli** | <span class="ar">ـُو</span> · <span class="ar">ـِي</span> | <span class="ar">نُور</span> · <span class="ar">دِين</span> |
| Oldida **boshqa harakat** yoki o'zida harakat bor | **undosh** | — | <span class="ar">وَرْد</span> · <span class="ar">زَيْت</span> |

**Taqqoslang:**

| So'z | <span class="ar">و</span> / <span class="ar">ي</span> nima | O'qilishi |
|---|---|---|
| <span class="ar">نُور</span> | damma + <span class="ar">و</span> → cho'ziq **ū** | nūr |
| <span class="ar">وَرْد</span> | <span class="ar">و</span> da fatha bor → undosh **w** | ward |
| <span class="ar">دِين</span> | kasra + <span class="ar">ي</span> → cho'ziq **ī** | dīn |
| <span class="ar">زَيْت</span> | fatha + <span class="ar">ي</span> sokin → **ay** | zayt |

> Oxirgi qator alohida holat: fatha + sokin <span class="ar">و</span> yoki <span class="ar">ي</span> → **ay** va **aw** qo'shma tovushlari. <span class="ar">ثَوْب</span> = thawb.

### 8.1 — Yangi harflar, uch harakat bilan

<div class="ar-blok">زَ &nbsp; زِ &nbsp; زُ &nbsp;&nbsp;|&nbsp;&nbsp; وَ &nbsp; وِ &nbsp; وُ</div>

### 8.2 — Qisqa va cho'ziq juftlar

<div class="ar-blok">دَ دَا &nbsp;&nbsp; دُ دُو &nbsp;&nbsp; دِ دِي &nbsp;&nbsp; رَ رَا &nbsp;&nbsp; نُ نُو &nbsp;&nbsp; بِ بِي</div>

### 8.3 — <span class="ar">ذ</span> va <span class="ar">ز</span> ni ajratish

Ketma-ket, sekin o'qing. Ikki tovush **boshqa** ekanini his qiling:

<div class="ar-blok">ذَ زَ &nbsp;&nbsp; ذِ زِ &nbsp;&nbsp; ذُ زُ &nbsp;&nbsp; ذَا زَا</div>

| Arabcha | O'qilishi | Ma'nosi |
|---|---|---|
| <span class="ar">دِين</span> | dīn | din |
| <span class="ar">نُور</span> | nūr | nur, yorug'lik |
| <span class="ar">وَرْد</span> | ward | atirgul |
| <span class="ar">بَرِيد</span> | barīd | pochta |
| <span class="ar">ذَنْب</span> | dhanb | gunoh |
| <span class="ar">تُرَاب</span> | turāb | tuproq |
| <span class="ar">زَيْت</span> | zayt | moy, yog' |
| <span class="ar">دِينَار</span> | dīnār | dinor |
| <span class="ar">تَدْبِير</span> | tadbīr | tadbir, chora |
| <span class="ar">رِبَا</span> | ribā | ribo, sudxo'rlik |

### ⭐ Diqqat qiling

<span class="ar">ثَوْب</span> (thawb — kiyim) va <span class="ar">تَوْب</span> — birinchi harfning nuqtasi 3 ta yoki 2 ta bo'lishiga qarab **butunlay boshqa so'z**. 1-darsdagi qoida kuchida qolmoqda: **nuqta — harfning o'zi, bezak emas**.

| Arabcha | Arabchada | O'zbekchada | Holat |
|---|---|---|---|
| <span class="ar">دِين</span> | din | din | ✅ Bir xil |
| <span class="ar">نُور</span> | yorug'lik | nur | ✅ Bir xil |
| <span class="ar">ذَنْب</span> | gunoh | zanb | ✅ Bir xil |
| <span class="ar">تُرَاب</span> | tuproq | turob | ✅ Bir xil |
| <span class="ar">دِينَار</span> | dinor (pul) | dinor | ✅ Bir xil |

> 💡 Sakkiztasining ettitasi **aynan** bir xil. Bu tasodif emas — bu so'zlar o'zbek tiliga arab tilidan kirgan. Roadmapdagi «500 so'zning 150 tasini bugun bilasiz» degani shu.

---

## 11. Yozuv mashqi

1. Daftarni **o'ng tomondan** oching
2. Har harfni **ikki shaklda** 10 martadan yozing (5 harf)
3. <span class="ar">ر</span> va <span class="ar">ز</span> ni yozganda **chiziqdan pastga tushishiga** e'tibor bering — <span class="ar">د</span> va <span class="ar">ذ</span> tushmaydi
4. 10 ta so'zni harakatlari bilan 5 martadan yozing
5. Nuqtalarni **oxirida** qo'ying

**Tekshirish** — yoddan yozing:

<div class="ar-blok">دِين &nbsp;&nbsp; نُور &nbsp;&nbsp; بَرِيد &nbsp;&nbsp; تُرَاب</div>

---

## 12. Tipik xatolar

| ❌ Xato | Nega bo'ladi | ✅ To'g'risi |
|---|---|---|
| <span class="ar">ذ</span> ni «z» deb o'qish | O'zbekcha odat (zanb, azon) | Til tishlar **orasiga**, inglizcha *this* |
| <span class="ar">ذ</span> va <span class="ar">ز</span> ni bir xil o'qish | Ikkalasi «z» ga o'xshaydi | Bular **ikki boshqa harf** |
| <span class="ar">د</span> va <span class="ar">ر</span> ni chalkashtirish | Shakli o'xshash | <span class="ar">ر</span> chiziqdan **pastga tushadi** |
| So'z ichidagi uzilishni bo'shliq deb bilish | 6 harf ulanmaydi | Uzilish **tor**, bo'shliq **keng** |
| Cho'zuvchi harfga harakat qo'yish | Odat bo'yicha har harfga qo'yiladi | Cho'zuvchi <span class="ar">ا و ي</span> — **harakatsiz** |
| <span class="ar">نُور</span> ni «nur» deb qisqa o'qish | O'zbekchada qisqa | **nūr** — ikki barobar uzun |
| <span class="ar">و</span> ni o'zbekcha «v» deb aytish | Yozuvda «v» deb beriladi | Lablar yumaloq, tishga **tegmaydi** |

---

## 13. Uy vazifasi

☐ Har harfni 2 shaklda 10 martadan yozish (5 harf)
☐ 10 ta so'zni harakatlari bilan 5 martadan yozish
☐ §8.3 mashqini (<span class="ar">ذ</span>/<span class="ar">ز</span>) ovoz chiqarib 3 marta o'qish
☐ 5 ta yangi kartochka — old tomonda harf, orqada: nomi · nuqtasi · tovushi · **ulanmasligi**
☐ 1-darsning kartochkalarini ham takrorlash (endi 11 ta bo'ldi)
☐ Har so'zni **bo'laklarga ajratib** yozish — §5 jadvalidagi kabi

---

## 14. O'z-o'zini tekshirish

☐ 6 ta ulanmaydigan harfni yoddan sanay olaman
☐ Bu harflar nega faqat 2 shaklda ekanini tushuntira olaman
☐ <span class="ar">د</span>/<span class="ar">ذ</span> va <span class="ar">ر</span>/<span class="ar">ز</span> juftliklarini ajrataman
☐ <span class="ar">ذ</span> ni <span class="ar">ز</span> dan **tovush jihatidan** farqlayman
☐ Uch cho'ziq unlining qanday hosil bo'lishini bilaman
☐ <span class="ar">نُور</span> da <span class="ar">و</span> unli, <span class="ar">وَرْد</span> da undosh ekanini **sababi bilan** aytaman
☐ 10 ta so'zni qaramasdan o'qiy olaman
☐ So'z ichidagi uzilishni so'z chegarasidan ajrataman
☐ 1-darsning 6 harfini hamon eslayman

**Agar 2 tadan ko'p ☐ qolsa** — darsni qaytaring.
