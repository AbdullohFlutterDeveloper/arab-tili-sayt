# 4-dars · <span class="ar">س ش ص ض</span> guruhi va bo'g'iq harflar

| | |
|---|---|
| 📚 **Bosqich** | 0 — Yozuv va o'qish ([[Arab tili Roadmap]] §0.1–0.3) |
| ⏱ **Vaqt** | 80–100 daqiqa · **2 kunga bo'ling** |
| 🎯 **Natija** | 4 yangi harf · **tafxim** tushunchasi · 18 harf · 10 ta yangi so'z |
| 📋 **Talab** | [[03-dars — Jim guruhi, shadda va tanvin\|3-dars]] |

> Bu darsda arab tilining o'zbek tilida **umuman yo'q** xususiyati ochiladi: bir xil tovushning **yengil** va **bo'g'iq** juftligi. Bu — 0-bosqichning eng muhim tushunchasi.

---

## 1. Ikki juftlik, ikki shakl

| Harf | Nomi | Nuqta | Tovush | O'zbekchada |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| <span class="ar">س</span> | <span class="ar">سِين</span> · sin | yo'q | **s** | ✅ bor |
| <span class="ar">ش</span> | <span class="ar">شِين</span> · shin | 3 ta, ustida | **sh** | ✅ bor |
| <span class="ar">ص</span> | <span class="ar">صَاد</span> · sod | yo'q | **ṣ** bo'g'iq | ❌ **yo'q** 🔴 |
| <span class="ar">ض</span> | <span class="ar">ضَاد</span> · zod | 1 ta, ustida | **ḍ** bo'g'iq | ❌ **yo'q** 🔴 |

<div class="ar-blok">س ← ش &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; ص ← ض</div>

<span class="ar">س</span> va <span class="ar">ش</span> — **uch tishli** asos. <span class="ar">ص</span> va <span class="ar">ض</span> — **kosali** asos. Har juftlikda farq faqat nuqtada.

---

## 2. 🔴 Tafxim — bo'g'iq harflar

Arab tilida ba'zi harflar juft-juft bo'lib keladi: biri **yengil** (<span class="ar">مُرَقَّق</span>), ikkinchisi **bo'g'iq** (<span class="ar">مُفَخَّم</span>).

**Nima farq qiladi:** tovush hosil bo'ladigan joy bir xil, lekin bo'g'iq harfda **til orqasi ko'tariladi** va tovush «yo'g'onlashadi». Yonidagi unli ham o'zgaradi.

| Yengil | Bo'g'iq | Farqi |
|---|---|---|
| <span class="ar">س</span> | <span class="ar">ص</span> | ikkalasi «s», lekin <span class="ar">ص</span> yo'g'on |
| <span class="ar">د</span> | <span class="ar">ض</span> | ikkalasi «d», lekin <span class="ar">ض</span> yo'g'on |

**Sinab ko'ring:**

<div class="ar-blok">سَ صَ &nbsp;&nbsp; سِ صِ &nbsp;&nbsp; سُ صُ</div>

<div class="ar-blok">دَ ضَ &nbsp;&nbsp; دِ ضِ &nbsp;&nbsp; دُ ضُ</div>

<span class="ar">سَ</span> deganda «sa» — yengil, ingichka. <span class="ar">صَ</span> deganda «so» ga yaqin — og'iz ichi kengayadi, tovush to'ladi.

> 🔴 **O'zbek uchun eng katta tuzoq:** <span class="ar">س</span> va <span class="ar">ص</span> ni bir xil «s» deb o'qish. O'zbek tilida bu farq yo'q, shuning uchun quloq uni **eshitmaydi** — avval **ongli ravishda** ajratish kerak.

> ⚠️ <span class="ar">ض</span> arab tilining eng o'ziga xos harfi. Arab tili hatto <span class="ar">لُغَة الضَّاد</span> — «zod tili» deb ataladi, chunki bu tovush boshqa tillarda deyarli uchramaydi.

O'zbek tilida bu harflar odatda oddiy «s» va «z» bilan yozilgan: <span class="ar">صَبْر</span> → sabr, <span class="ar">ضَرْب</span> → zarb. Bu **yozuv qulayligi**, talaffuz emas.

## 7. Yozuv mashqi

1. Har harfni 4 shaklda 10 martadan
2. <span class="ar">س</span> ning **uch tishini** aniq yozing — <span class="ar">ـبـ</span> dan farqi shu
3. <span class="ar">ص</span> va <span class="ar">ض</span> ning kosasini yumaloq, dumini uzun qiling
4. 10 ta so'zni harakatlari bilan 5 martadan
5. Juftliklarni **yonma-yon** yozing: <span class="ar">سَ صَ</span> · <span class="ar">دَ ضَ</span>

**Tekshirish** — yoddan:

<div class="ar-blok">دَرْس &nbsp;&nbsp; صَبْر &nbsp;&nbsp; شَخْص &nbsp;&nbsp; رِضَا</div>

## 8. Tipik xatolar

| ❌ Xato | Nega bo'ladi | ✅ To'g'risi |
|---|---|---|
| <span class="ar">س</span> va <span class="ar">ص</span> ni bir xil o'qish | O'zbekchada farq yo'q | <span class="ar">ص</span> — til orqasi **ko'tariladi** |
| <span class="ar">د</span> va <span class="ar">ض</span> ni bir xil o'qish | Yozuvda ikkalasi «z»/«d» | <span class="ar">ض</span> **yo'g'on** |
| <span class="ar">س</span> ni <span class="ar">ـبـ</span> deb o'qish | Tishlar o'xshash | <span class="ar">س</span> da **uch** tish, nuqtasiz |
| <span class="ar">ش</span> nuqtalarini 2 ta qo'yish | <span class="ar">ت</span> bilan chalkashish | <span class="ar">ش</span> da **3 ta**, uchburchak shaklida |
| Bo'g'iqlikni «urg'u» deb o'ylash | — | Bu **til holati**, ovoz balandligi emas |
| <span class="ar">ضَرْب</span> ni «zarb» deb aytish | O'zbekcha shakl | Arabchada **ḍarb**, «d» ga yaqin |

## 9. Uy vazifasi

☐ Har harfni 4 shaklda 10 martadan yozish (4 harf)
☐ 10 ta so'zni harakatlari bilan 5 martadan
☐ §4.2 juftlik mashqini ovoz chiqarib 3 marta — **o'zingizni yozib oling**
☐ 4 ta yangi kartochka + tafxim uchun bitta tushuncha kartochkasi
