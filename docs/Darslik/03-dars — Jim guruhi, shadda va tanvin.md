# A qism — <span class="ar">ج ح خ</span> guruhi

## 1. Yana bitta shakl, uchta harf

1-darsdagi mantiq takrorlanadi: **bitta asos, nuqta bilan farq**.

| Harf | Nomi | Nuqta | Tovush | O'zbekchada |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| <span class="ar">ج</span> | <span class="ar">جِيم</span> · jim | 1 ta, **ichida** | **j** | ✅ bor |
| <span class="ar">ح</span> | <span class="ar">حَاء</span> · ho | **yo'q** | **ḥ** | ❌ **yo'q** 🔴 |
| <span class="ar">خ</span> | <span class="ar">خَاء</span> · xo | 1 ta, **ustida** | **kh** | ✅ bor |

<div class="ar-blok">ح ← ج &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; ح ← خ</div>

Nuqtasiz <span class="ar">ح</span> — guruhning «asosi». Nuqta ichiga tushsa <span class="ar">ج</span>, ustiga chiqsa <span class="ar">خ</span>.

---

## 2. 🔴 <span class="ar">ح</span> — darsning eng qiyin qismi

Roadmap buni alohida qizil bilan belgilagan: **o'zbek tilida bu tovush yo'q**, va noto'g'ri o'rnashsa tuzatish juda qiyin.

| Harf | Qayerdan chiqadi | O'zbekcha «h» bilan farqi |
|---|---|---|
| <span class="ar">ه</span> | tomoqning **eng chuqur**, erkin | «havo» dagi h — bu yengil |
| <span class="ar">ح</span> | tomoq **o'rtasi**, **siqiladi** | 🔴 Ancha kuchli, «xirillamaydi» |
| <span class="ar">خ</span> | tomoq **yuqorisi**, xirillaydi | «xat» dagi x — bu bor |

**Amaliy usul:** og'zingizni oching, tomoqni ozgina siqing va **isitgandek** «hhh» chiqaring — lekin <span class="ar">خ</span> dagi xirillash **bo'lmasin**. <span class="ar">ح</span> — toza, siqilgan nafas.

> 🔴 Eng ko'p uchraydigan xato: <span class="ar">ح</span> ni o'zbekcha «h» deb aytish. Natijada <span class="ar">بَحْر</span> (dengiz) va boshqa so'zlar noto'g'ri chiqadi. **Bu tovushni videodan eshitmasdan o'tmang** — dars oxiridagi havolaga qarang.

---

## 3. To'rt shakl

| Harf | Alohida | Boshda | O'rtada | Oxirida |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| jim | <span class="ar">ج</span> | <span class="ar">جـ</span> | <span class="ar">ـجـ</span> | <span class="ar">ـج</span> |
| ho | <span class="ar">ح</span> | <span class="ar">حـ</span> | <span class="ar">ـحـ</span> | <span class="ar">ـح</span> |
| xo | <span class="ar">خ</span> | <span class="ar">خـ</span> | <span class="ar">ـخـ</span> | <span class="ar">ـخ</span> |

⭐ E'tibor bering: **alohida va oxirgi** shaklda bu harflar chiziqdan **pastga tushadi** — xuddi <span class="ar">ن</span> va <span class="ar">ي</span> kabi. Boshda va o'rtada esa tushmaydi.

<div class="ar-blok">جـ ـجـ ـج ج</div>

---

## 4. O'qish mashqi — A qism

<div class="ar-blok">جَ &nbsp; جِ &nbsp; جُ &nbsp;&nbsp;|&nbsp;&nbsp; حَ &nbsp; حِ &nbsp; حُ &nbsp;&nbsp;|&nbsp;&nbsp; خَ &nbsp; خِ &nbsp; خُ</div>

Uch tovushni **ketma-ket** aytib, farqni his qiling:

<div class="ar-blok">جَا حَا خَا &nbsp;&nbsp; جِي حِي خِي &nbsp;&nbsp; جُو حُو خُو</div>

# B qism — <span class="ar">الشَّدَّة</span> shadda

## 5. Shadda — undoshni ikkilantiradi

| Belgi | Nomi | Joyi | Ma'nosi |
|---|---|---|---|
| <span class="ar">ـّ</span> | <span class="ar">شَدَّة</span> · shadda | harf **ustida** | undosh **ikki marta** aytiladi |

Shaddali harf = **sokin harf + harakatli harf**, ikkisi birga yozilgan:

<div class="ar-blok">رَبْ + بَ &nbsp;←&nbsp; رَبَّ</div>

**Harakat qayerda turadi?** Shadda **ustida**, harakat esa odatdagi joyida — fatha shadda ustiga, kasra pastga:

| Yozuv | O'qilishi |
|---|---|
| <span class="ar">بَّ</span> | bba |
| <span class="ar">بِّ</span> | bbi |
| <span class="ar">بُّ</span> | bbu |

### Shaddali so'zlar

| Arabcha | O'qilishi | Ma'nosi |
|---|---|---|
| <span class="ar">رَبّ</span> | rabb | Rabb, Parvardigor |
| <span class="ar">حَجّ</span> | ḥajj | haj |
| <span class="ar">حُبّ</span> | ḥubb | sevgi |
| <span class="ar">جَدّ</span> | jadd | bobo |

> 🔴 **Shaddani tashlab ketish — ma'no buzadi.** Uni «bezak» deb o'ylamang: ikkilangan undosh **eshitiladigan darajada** cho'ziladi.

# C qism — <span class="ar">التَّنْوِين</span> tanvin

## 6. Tanvin — harakatning ikkilanishi

Tanvin — **harakat belgisining ikki marta yozilishi**. Talaffuzda oxiriga **«n»** qo'shiladi.

| Belgi | Nomi | Tovush | Misol | O'qilishi |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| <span class="ar">ـً</span> | tanvin fatha | **-an** | <span class="ar">بَيْتًا</span> | baytan |
| <span class="ar">ـٍ</span> | tanvin kasra | **-in** | <span class="ar">بَيْتٍ</span> | baytin |
| <span class="ar">ـٌ</span> | tanvin damma | **-un** | <span class="ar">بَيْتٌ</span> | baytun |

**Qoida:** tanvin faqat so'zning **oxirida** bo'ladi. So'z o'rtasida hech qachon uchramaydi.

<div class="ar-blok">خَبَرٌ &nbsp;&nbsp; خَبَرًا &nbsp;&nbsp; خَبَرٍ</div>

> ⭐ Tanvin fatha yozilganda odatda oxiriga **alif** qo'shiladi: <span class="ar">خَبَرًا</span>. Bu alif **o'qilmaydi** — u faqat tanvinning tayanchi.

**Nima uchun kerak?** Tanvin — so'zning **noaniq** ekanini bildiradi. 1-darsda ko'rgan edik: tanvin — ismning alomatlaridan biri. Uning grammatik ma'nosini keyingi bosqichlarda ochamiz; hozircha **o'qiy olish** kifoya.

**O'ntadan to'qqiztasi** o'zbek tilida bor. Bu — roadmapdagi ustunlikning eng yaqqol namunasi:

## 9. O'qish mashqi — hammasi birga

Shadda va tanvin bilan:

<div class="ar-blok">خَبَرٌ &nbsp;&nbsp; حَدِيثٌ &nbsp;&nbsp; تَاجِرٌ &nbsp;&nbsp; بَحْرٌ</div>

<div class="ar-blok">رَبٌّ &nbsp;&nbsp; حُبٌّ &nbsp;&nbsp; جَدٌّ &nbsp;&nbsp; حَجٌّ</div>

<span class="ar">ح</span> va <span class="ar">خ</span> ni ajratish — sekin, ovoz chiqarib:

<div class="ar-blok">بَحْر خَيْر &nbsp;&nbsp; حَبِيب خَبَر &nbsp;&nbsp; نَجَاح جَدِيد</div>

---

## 10. Yozuv mashqi

1. <span class="ar">ج ح خ</span> — har birini 4 shaklda 10 martadan
2. Alohida va oxirgi shaklda **chiziqdan pastga tushishiga** e'tibor bering
3. Shaddali 4 so'zni 5 martadan
4. Bitta so'zni **uch tanvin** bilan yozing: <span class="ar">خَبَرٌ · خَبَرًا · خَبَرٍ</span>
5. 10 ta yangi so'zni harakatlari bilan 5 martadan

**Tekshirish** — yoddan:

<div class="ar-blok">حَدِيث &nbsp;&nbsp; خَبَر &nbsp;&nbsp; بَحْر &nbsp;&nbsp; حُبّ</div>

---

## 11. Tipik xatolar

| ❌ Xato | Nega bo'ladi | ✅ To'g'risi |
|---|---|---|
| <span class="ar">ح</span> ni o'zbekcha «h» deb aytish | O'zbekchada faqat bitta «h» bor | Tomoq **siqiladi**, xirillash yo'q |
| <span class="ar">ح</span> va <span class="ar">خ</span> ni aralashtirish | Shakli bir xil | <span class="ar">خ</span> **xirillaydi**, <span class="ar">ح</span> yo'q |
| <span class="ar">ج</span> nuqtasini ustiga qo'yish | <span class="ar">خ</span> bilan chalkashadi | <span class="ar">ج</span> nuqtasi **ichida** |
| Shaddani o'qimaslik | Kichik belgi, ko'zdan qochadi | Undosh **ikki marta** aytiladi |
| Tanvin alifini o'qish | Alif ko'rinib turadi | <span class="ar">ـًا</span> dagi alif **jim** |
| Tanvinni so'z o'rtasida kutish | — | Tanvin **faqat oxirida** |
| <span class="ar">جَدِيد</span> ni «jadid harakati» deb tarjima qilish | O'zbekcha ma'no | Arabchada shunchaki **«yangi»** |

---

## 12. Uy vazifasi

☐ <span class="ar">ج ح خ</span> — har birini 4 shaklda 10 martadan yozish
☐ Shaddali 4 so'z + 10 ta yangi so'z, harakatlari bilan
☐ <span class="ar">خَبَر</span> so'zini uch tanvin bilan yozish
☐ §9 mashqlarini ovoz chiqarib 3 martadan o'qish
☐ 3 ta yangi harf kartochkasi + shadda va tanvin uchun 2 ta belgi kartochkasi
☐ 1- va 2-dars kartochkalarini takrorlash (**endi 14 harf**)
☐ 🔴 <span class="ar">ح</span> talaffuzini videodan eshitib, o'zingizni **yozib olib** solishtiring

---

## 13. O'z-o'zini tekshirish

☐ <span class="ar">ج ح خ</span> ni nuqtasi bo'yicha ajrataman
☐ <span class="ar">ح</span> ni o'zbekcha «h» dan **tovush jihatidan** farqlayman
☐ <span class="ar">ح</span> va <span class="ar">خ</span> ni ketma-ket to'g'ri ayta olaman
☐ Shadda nima qilishini va uni qanday o'qishni bilaman
☐ Uch tanvinni belgisiga qarab ayta olaman
☐ Tanvin fathadagi alif **o'qilmasligini** bilaman
☐ 10 ta yangi so'zni qaramasdan o'qiy olaman
☐ <span class="ar">حَدِيث</span> so'zidagi **to'rt** harfni nomi bilan ayta olaman
☐ 1- va 2-darsdagi 11 harfni hamon eslayman

**Agar 2 tadan ko'p ☐ qolsa** — darsni qaytaring. Ayniqsa <span class="ar">ح</span> bandi ☐ bo'lsa, oldinga yurmang.
