# 6-dars · Alifbo yakuni — <span class="ar">ف ق ك ل م ه</span>

| | |
|---|---|
| 📚 **Bosqich** | 0 — Yozuv va o'qish ([[Arab tili Roadmap]] §0.1) |
| ⏱ **Vaqt** | 100–120 daqiqa · **3 kunga bo'ling** |
| 🎯 **Natija** | 🏁 **28 harfning hammasi** · <span class="ar">ة</span> · <span class="ar">لا</span> birikmasi · 10 ta yangi so'z |
| 📋 **Talab** | [[05-dars — Ayn guruhi va tafximning davomi\|5-dars]] |

> 🏁 **Bu — alifboning oxirgi darsi.** Oltita harf qoldi va ularning **hammasi o'zbek tilida bor**. Eng qiyin qism ortda: <span class="ar">ع ح ث ذ ص ض ط ظ</span> — hammasini o'tdingiz.

---

## 1. Oxirgi olti harf

| Harf | Nomi | Nuqta | Tovush | O'zbekchada |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| <span class="ar">ف</span> | <span class="ar">فَاء</span> · fo | 1 ta, ustida | **f** | ✅ bor |
| <span class="ar">ق</span> | <span class="ar">قَاف</span> · qof | 2 ta, ustida | **q** | ✅ bor |
| <span class="ar">ك</span> | <span class="ar">كَاف</span> · kof | yo'q | **k** | ✅ bor |
| <span class="ar">ل</span> | <span class="ar">لَام</span> · lom | yo'q | **l** | ✅ bor |
| <span class="ar">م</span> | <span class="ar">مِيم</span> · mim | yo'q | **m** | ✅ bor |
| <span class="ar">ه</span> | <span class="ar">هَاء</span> · ha | yo'q | **h** | ✅ bor |

> ⭐ Oltitasining **hammasi** o'zbek tilida mavjud. Roadmapdagi <span class="ar">ق</span> haqidagi izoh: *«Deyarli bir xil»* — «qalam» dagi q aynan shu.

### <span class="ar">ف</span> va <span class="ar">ق</span> — yana bir nuqta juftligi

<div class="ar-blok">ف ← ق</div>

Shakli o'xshash, farq nuqtada. Lekin **alohida va oxirgi** shaklda ajratish oson:
<span class="ar">ق</span> chiziqdan **pastga tushadi**, <span class="ar">ف</span> **tushmaydi**.

---

## 2. 🔴 <span class="ar">ه</span> — to'rt shakli bir-biriga o'xshamaydi

Bu — alifbodagi eng «o'zgaruvchan» harf. To'rt shaklni **alohida** yodlash kerak:

<div class="ar-blok">هـ &nbsp;&nbsp; ـهـ &nbsp;&nbsp; ـه &nbsp;&nbsp; ه</div>

| Shakl | Ko'rinishi |
|---|---|
| Boshda | <span class="ar">هـ</span> — ikki halqa |
| O'rtada | <span class="ar">ـهـ</span> — ko'zga o'xshaydi |
| Oxirida | <span class="ar">ـه</span> — ilmoq |
| Alohida | <span class="ar">ه</span> — yumaloq |

> ⚠️ Boshlovchilar <span class="ar">ـهـ</span> ni ko'rib «bu qaysi harf?» deb qotib qoladi. Oldindan biling: bu <span class="ar">ه</span>.

## 4. <span class="ar">لا</span> — majburiy birikma

<span class="ar">ل</span> dan keyin <span class="ar">ا</span> kelsa, ular **qo'shilib bitta shaklga** aylanadi:

<div class="ar-blok">ل + ا &nbsp;←&nbsp; لا</div>

Bu — **tanlov emas, qoida**. <span class="ar">ل</span> va <span class="ar">ا</span> ni yonma-yon alohida yozib bo'lmaydi.

| Shakl | Qachon |
|---|---|
| <span class="ar">لا</span> | oldingi harfga ulanmaganda |
| <span class="ar">ـلا</span> | oldingi harfga ulanganda |

⚠️ <span class="ar">لا</span> dan keyin **uzilish bo'ladi**, chunki ichidagi alif ulanmaydi (2-dars qoidasi).

---

## 5. <span class="ar">ة</span> — <span class="ar">تَاء مَرْبُوطَة</span>

Bu — alifbodagi 28 harfdan **tashqari** belgi, lekin har kuni uchraydi.

| Belgi | Nomi | Shakllari | Qayerda |
|---|---|---|---|
| <span class="ar">ة</span> | tai marbuta | <span class="ar">ة</span> · <span class="ar">ـة</span> | **faqat so'z oxirida** |

**Ko'rinishi:** <span class="ar">ه</span> ga o'xshaydi, lekin ustida <span class="ar">ت</span> ning **ikki nuqtasi** bor.

**Qanday o'qiladi** — ikki holat:

| Holat | Tovush | Misol |
|---|---|---|
| So'zda **to'xtasangiz** | **h** | <span class="ar">كَلِمَة</span> → kalimah |
| Davom etsangiz | **t** | <span class="ar">كَلِمَةُ</span> → kalimatu |

> ⭐ <span class="ar">ة</span> — ismning **ayollik** belgisi. Grammatik ma'nosini keyingi bosqichda ochamiz; hozircha o'qiy olish kifoya.

## 9. Yozuv mashqi

1. Har harfni 4 shaklda 10 martadan (<span class="ar">ه</span> — 15 martadan)
2. <span class="ar">لا</span> birikmasini 15 marta
3. <span class="ar">ة</span> ni so'z oxirida 10 marta
4. 10 ta so'zni harakatlari bilan 5 martadan
5. 🏁 **Butun alifboni** boshidan oxirigacha bir marta yozib chiqing

**Tekshirish** — yoddan:

<div class="ar-blok">كِتَاب &nbsp;&nbsp; مَسْجِد &nbsp;&nbsp; كَلِمَة &nbsp;&nbsp; مَدْرَسَة</div>

## 11. Uy vazifasi

## 12. 🏁 Bosqich 0 — yakuniy tekshiruv

Bu — oddiy dars tekshiruvi emas, **butun alifbo bo'yicha darvoza**:

☐ 28 harfni **tartib bilan** ayta olaman
☐ Har harfning 4 shaklini tanidim
☐ 6 ta ulanmaydigan harfni yoddan bilaman
☐ 4 ta bo'g'iq harfni va ularning yengil juftini bilaman
☐ Nuqta bilan farqlanuvchi 9 guruhni ajrataman
☐ <span class="ar">ه</span> ning to'rt shaklini tanidim
☐ <span class="ar">ة</span> ni <span class="ar">ه</span> dan ajrataman
☐ <span class="ar">لا</span> birikmasini bilaman
☐ Fatha, kasra, damma, sukun, shadda, tanvinni o'qiy olaman
☐ Uch cho'ziq unlini bilaman
☐ 🔴 <span class="ar">ع</span> va <span class="ar">ح</span> ni to'g'ri chiqara olaman
☐ 6 darsdagi **60 ta so'zni** qaramasdan o'qiy olaman
☐ Harakatli notanish matnni sekin bo'lsa ham o'qiy olaman

> **Hammasi ☑ bo'lsa** — alifbo tugadi. Endi 0-bosqichning qolgan qismi: <span class="ar">ال</span> aniqlik artikli, hamza, va tajvid asoslari.
>
> **2 tadan ko'p ☐ qolsa** — roadmapdagi qoida: *«Bosqich 0 ni hech qanday holatda o'tkazib yubormang.»* Qaytaring.
