## 2. Tafximning davomi — <span class="ar">ط</span> va <span class="ar">ظ</span>

4-darsda ikki juftlikni ko'rdik. Endi qolgan ikkitasi:

| Yengil | Bo'g'iq | Farqi |
|---|---|---|
| <span class="ar">ت</span> | <span class="ar">ط</span> | ikkalasi «t», <span class="ar">ط</span> yo'g'on |
| <span class="ar">ذ</span> | <span class="ar">ظ</span> | ikkalasi tishlararo, <span class="ar">ظ</span> yo'g'on |

<div class="ar-blok">تَ طَ &nbsp;&nbsp; تِ طِ &nbsp;&nbsp; تُ طُ</div>

<div class="ar-blok">ذَ ظَ &nbsp;&nbsp; ذِ ظِ &nbsp;&nbsp; ذُ ظُ</div>

**To'liq tafxim juftliklari** — endi hammasi qo'lingizda:

| Yengil | <span class="ar">س</span> | <span class="ar">د</span> | <span class="ar">ت</span> | <span class="ar">ذ</span> |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bo'g'iq** | <span class="ar">ص</span> | <span class="ar">ض</span> | <span class="ar">ط</span> | <span class="ar">ظ</span> |

> ⭐ To'rt bo'g'iq harf: <span class="ar">ص ض ط ظ</span>. Ularni **birga** yodlang — tajvidda ham shu to'rtlik alohida guruh bo'lib yuradi.

---

## 3. 🔴🔴 <span class="ar">ع</span> — alifboning eng qiyin harfi

Roadmapda aynan shunday yozilgan: **«Eng qiyini. Tomoq siqiladi. Ustoz yoki video shart.»**

**Qayerdan chiqadi:** tomoqning **o'rtasidan**, <span class="ar">ح</span> bilan **bir joydan** — lekin <span class="ar">ع</span> da **ovoz bor**.

| Harf | Joyi | Ovoz |
|---|---|---|
| <span class="ar">ح</span> | tomoq o'rtasi | ❌ yo'q (nafas) |
| <span class="ar">ع</span> | tomoq o'rtasi | ✅ **bor** |

Ya'ni <span class="ar">ع</span> — 3-darsda o'rgangan <span class="ar">ح</span> ning **ovozli jufti**. Xuddi <span class="ar">ث</span>/<span class="ar">ذ</span> juftligi kabi.

**Amaliy usul:**
1. <span class="ar">ح</span> ni chiqaring — tomoq siqiladi, nafas o'tadi
2. Xuddi shu holatni saqlab, **ovoz qo'shing**
3. Tomoq siqilgan holda «aaa» deyilsa — <span class="ar">عَ</span> chiqadi

> 🔴 **Eng ko'p uchraydigan xato:** <span class="ar">ع</span> ni **umuman aytmaslik** — uni oddiy unliga aylantirib yuborish. <span class="ar">عَرَب</span> ni «arab» deb aytish shunday. Arabchada boshida **tomoq tovushi** bor.

> ⚠️ Bu harfni **faqat matndan** o'rganib bo'lmaydi. Dars oxiridagi videoni ko'ring va o'z ovozingizni yozib solishtiring. Bu — 0-bosqichda ustoz eng zarur bo'lgan yagona nuqta.

### <span class="ar">غ</span> — yengilroq

<span class="ar">غ</span> o'zbek tilida **bor**: «g'alaba», «g'oz» dagi tovush. Bu darsdagi yagona oson harf. U <span class="ar">خ</span> ning ovozli jufti.

|---|---|---|---|---|

⚠️ **Ikki muhim kuzatuv:**

**<span class="ar">ط</span> va <span class="ar">ظ</span>** — shakli deyarli **o'zgarmaydi**. Bu alifbodagi eng barqaror ikki harf.

**<span class="ar">ع</span> va <span class="ar">غ</span>** — aksincha, shakli **eng ko'p o'zgaradigan** harflar. To'rt shakli bir-biriga o'xshamaydi:

<div class="ar-blok">عـ &nbsp;&nbsp; ـعـ &nbsp;&nbsp; ـع &nbsp;&nbsp; ع</div>

Bu to'rttasini **alohida-alohida** yodlash kerak.

## 7. O'zbekchada tanish so'zlar

Yana **o'ntasining hammasi** tanish. Lekin diqqat: o'zbekcha shakl talaffuzni **yashiradi**.

| Arabcha | O'zbekchada | Nima yo'qolgan |
|---|---|---|
| <span class="ar">عَرَب</span> | arab | boshidagi <span class="ar">ع</span> |
| <span class="ar">طَبِيب</span> | tabib | <span class="ar">ط</span> ning bo'g'iqligi |
| <span class="ar">عَذَاب</span> | azob | <span class="ar">ع</span> va <span class="ar">ذ</span> |
| <span class="ar">غَضَب</span> | g'azab | <span class="ar">ض</span> ning bo'g'iqligi |
| <span class="ar">نَظَر</span> | nazar | <span class="ar">ظ</span> ning bo'g'iqligi |
| <span class="ar">عَصْر</span> | asr | <span class="ar">ع</span> va <span class="ar">ص</span> |
| <span class="ar">غَرِيب</span> | g'arib | — ✅ yaqin |
| <span class="ar">عَزِيز</span> | aziz | boshidagi <span class="ar">ع</span> |
| <span class="ar">شَرْط</span> | shart | <span class="ar">ط</span> ning bo'g'iqligi |
| <span class="ar">وَطَن</span> | vatan | <span class="ar">و</span> va <span class="ar">ط</span> |

> ⭐ **Bu jadval — darsning asosiy xulosasi.** So'zni bilasiz, lekin o'zbekcha shakli sizga **noto'g'ri talaffuz** o'rgatgan. Endi asl tovushni tiklashingiz kerak.

## 8. Yozuv mashqi

1. <span class="ar">ط</span> va <span class="ar">ظ</span> — kosani yozib, keyin **tik chiziqni** qo'ying
2. <span class="ar">ع</span> va <span class="ar">غ</span> — to'rt shaklni **alohida** 15 martadan
3. 10 ta so'zni harakatlari bilan 5 martadan
4. Tafximning to'rt juftligini yonma-yon yozing

**Tekshirish** — yoddan:

<div class="ar-blok">عَرَب &nbsp;&nbsp; عَصْر &nbsp;&nbsp; وَطَن &nbsp;&nbsp; نَظَر</div>

## 9. Tipik xatolar

| ❌ Xato | Nega bo'ladi | ✅ To'g'risi |
|---|---|---|
| <span class="ar">ع</span> ni umuman aytmaslik | O'zbekchada yo'q | Tomoq **siqiladi**, ovoz bilan |
| <span class="ar">ع</span> ni <span class="ar">ء</span> (hamza) kabi aytish | Ikkalasi ham tomoqda | <span class="ar">ع</span> — **davomli**, hamza — uzilish |
| <span class="ar">ع</span> va <span class="ar">غ</span> shakllarini chalkashtirish | 4 shakli juda farqli | Har shaklni **alohida** yodlang |
| <span class="ar">ط</span> ni oddiy «t» deb aytish | O'zbekcha «tabib» | <span class="ar">ط</span> — **bo'g'iq** |
| <span class="ar">ظ</span> ni «z» deb aytish | O'zbekcha «nazar» | Til tishlar **orasida** + bo'g'iq |
| <span class="ar">ظ</span> va <span class="ar">ض</span> ni aralashtirish | Ikkalasi bo'g'iq | <span class="ar">ظ</span> tishlararo, <span class="ar">ض</span> emas |

## 10. Uy vazifasi

☐ Har harfni 4 shaklda 10 martadan (<span class="ar">ع</span> va <span class="ar">غ</span> — 15 martadan)
☐ 10 ta so'zni harakatlari bilan 5 martadan
☐ §5.2 va §5.4 mashqlarini ovoz chiqarib 3 martadan
☐ 4 ta yangi kartochka
☐ 1–4-dars kartochkalarini takrorlash (**endi 22 harf**)
☐ 🔴🔴 <span class="ar">ع</span> talaffuzini videodan **kamida 10 marta** eshitib, ovozingizni yozib solishtiring
☐ §7 jadvalini qayta o'qing — har so'zda nima yo'qolganini ayting

## 11. O'z-o'zini tekshirish
